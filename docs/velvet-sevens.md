# Velvet Sevens

Velvet Sevens keeps its ruby sevens, fruit symbols and horseshoes in an Art Deco cabinet with garnet lacquer and champagne brass. The existing lounge scene and symbol sheet supply the background and symbols. The new cabinet shell is a transparent PNG generated with the built-in ImageGen tool using the scene as a visual reference.

The brass-framed openings contain live UI. React renders the marquee, credit and wager readout, five paylines and three independently stopping reels. The cabinet and the side console share a wager and call the same spin handler. The 40° lever springs back as soon as the player releases it, independently of the reels. Lights chase during play, and winning coins spill from the brass tray.

Three horseshoes award seven free spins at the triggering wager. The game ID remains `royal` for the server, routes, and saved history.

The cabinet image is stored at `client/public/assets/velvet-sevens-cabinet.png`. Its transparent edges are preserved. Its title, controls, reel symbols and glass reflections are drawn by the client.

## Lever hardware

The built-in ImageGen tool produced the transparent parts atlas at `client/public/assets/velvet-lever-hardware.png`. The right-facing bearing artwork at `client/public/assets/velvet-lever-side-bearing.png` provides the fixed backplate. The 3D hub is shown at a 20° angle to reveal its right-side end cap. Its sleeve and slotted cap turn through the same 40° pull as the rigid shaft and ruby. The brass and ruby use the original artwork as texture. The illustrated lever remains playable if WebGL cannot start.

